Peer-Reviewed Crop Disease Management Guides

Wheat Yellow / Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis)

Symptoms: Linear yellow-orange powdery pustules along leaf veins. Chemical control: Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1ml/L water or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1ml/L. Organic alternative: 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) + Trichoderma viride. Expert Reviewer: Dr. Surender K. Verma, ICAR Emeritus Fellow.

Cotton Leaf Curl Virus Complex (Begomovirus)

Symptoms: Upward leaf curling, thickened veins, enations, and stunted growth transmitted by whitefly (Bemisia tabaci). Chemical control: Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2g/L or Pyriproxyfen 10% EC @ 2ml/L. Organic alternative: 10 Yellow Sticky Traps/acre + 5% NSKE spray.

Rice Blast & Sheath Blight (Magnaporthe oryzae)

Symptoms: Spindle-shaped lesions with grayish centers and brown margins on paddy foliage. Chemical control: Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6g/L or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1ml/L.

Soybean Mosaic & Chlorosis Complex

Symptoms: Vein clearing, mosaic mottling, rugosity, and stunted trifoliates. Chemical control: Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 40g/acre + Chelated Zinc foliar nutrition.

Mustard White Rust & Alternaria Blight (Albugo candida)

Symptoms: Prominent white pustules on abaxial leaf surfaces and stag-head floral distortion. Chemical control: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/L or Ridomil MZ @ 2g/L water at 15-day intervals.
